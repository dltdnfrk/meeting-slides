renders the key input only when 'keyBased && !status.selectable'.

the key-entry affordance is the correct, non-redundant behavior.
  openai  -> badge '미설정',  hasKeyInput=true   (correct: needs a key)
  alibaba -> badge '설정됨',  hasKeyInput=false  (correct: already configured)
Verdict: PASS for the product; the QA assertion was mis-specified.
